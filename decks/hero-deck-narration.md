# Hero deck — Velo narration script

10 slides. ~85 seconds total at natural pace. Each slide gets one to two
sentences. Read with a slight rasp / lower pitch, not "podcast-bro." Pauses
between slides land naturally where Velo cuts.

## Setup in Velo

1. Upload `hero-deck.pdf` via "Upload PDFs & PNGs"
2. Pick a male voice (Velo "Owen" or "Reed" sound right for this brand)
3. Paste the per-slide narration below as the script when prompted, OR
   record it live with the canvas open

---

### Slide 1 — Title
> "Six production AI agents. Buy them once. Deploy them in minutes. And resell them to clients at fifteen hundred to three thousand dollars a month, every month."

### Slide 2 — Stop renting your stack
> "Right now you're probably paying Zapier, Make, Lindy, or Relevance fifty to three hundred dollars a month — per seat, every month — for a workflow that's basically wrapping an LLM call you could write yourself."

### Slide 3 — Own the agent, own the margin
> "The shift is simple. Source code on your GitHub. Runs on your infrastructure. Wears your brand. No per-seat ceiling. No rate-limit ransom. No founder selling to a bigger company and emailing you that they're sunsetting your stack."

### Slide 4 — Six agents
> "We ship six production-grade agents. Ads, sales, social, voice, SEO, and a multi-tool MCP pack. Already running across seven Shopify stores and nine production AI services. Not demo code — the same code we use ourselves."

### Slide 5 — Everything in the box
> "When you buy, you become a collaborator on a private GitHub repo with the full thing. The agent source. The dot-claude config we use to ship in production. Nine MCPs that Claude calls directly. Deploy recipes for every common stack. And brand-config schemas so you can re-skin the same agent for every client."

### Slide 6 — Twenty minutes from clone to live
> "Single-VM deploy is about twenty minutes from git clone to live. The kit ships systemd, nginx, GCE cloud-init, Cloud Run YAML, and Docker — pick whichever fits your stack. First client is a couple of hours. Client two onward is muscle memory."

### Slide 7 — Five clients, $7,500 MRR
> "The math is what closes most buyers. Five clients on one agent at fifteen hundred a month each puts you at seven and a half thousand recurring. One agent. One weekend. Recurring monthly revenue from day one."

### Slide 8 — Buy once, updates forever
> "And here's the part most people miss. You buy the agent once. But every patch we ship, every new agent we add, every new MCP we wire up — you get all of it, automatically, for life. No SaaS bill creeping up twenty percent a year. No 'migrate to version two' upgrade fee. No vendor acquisition wiping out your account."

### Slide 9 — Pricing
> "Founder Stack is four hundred ninety-nine dollars, one-time, for all six agents and the MCP pack. Or buy them à la carte from thirty-nine to one forty-nine each. Either way: lifetime updates, BSL license, rebrand and resell."

### Slide 10 — CTA
> "Stop building infra. Start shipping clients. Glitch Grow dot Executor dot com."
