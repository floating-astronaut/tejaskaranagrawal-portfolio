## Two layers, often confused

The outbound stack splits cleanly into a sender layer (Instantly, Smartlead, Lemlist) and an agent layer (research, drafting, approval, learning). Instantly is one of the better sender layers; the Sales Agent is purpose-built for the agent layer.

Pick the layer your bottleneck is on. New agencies with cold sender pools usually need Instantly first; established agencies with warm pools but inconsistent reply rates usually need the agent layer.
