## When Relevance AI is the right pick

Solo operators or small teams who don't want any infrastructure responsibility get real value from Relevance — fast time-to-first-agent, broad pre-built tool library, and a builder that's better than most. If those constraints describe you, the subscription is worth it.

## When agencies switch

The pivot point is almost always the same — an agency wants the agent branded as their own, billed by them, on infrastructure they control. That's hard to do cleanly through a managed platform. BSL 1.1 + brand-config JSON makes it native in Glitch Grow.
