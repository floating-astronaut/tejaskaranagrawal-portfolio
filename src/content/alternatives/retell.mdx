## Two camps in voice AI right now

Polished managed platforms (Vapi, Retell, Bland) and source-available stacks. The first camp gets you to first call faster; the second camp gets you to break-even unit economics at scale.

For Indian-language workloads specifically — where Sarvam outperforms generic STT on regional accents — owning the stack is also a quality argument, not just a cost one.
