## Two categories, often conflated

Smartlead, Instantly, Lemlist, Apollo Sequences — these are sequence runners. Their job is to deliver mail to inboxes reliably across rotating sender pools. They do that job well.

The Sales Agent's job is different: decide who to email, with which recipe, queue it for human review, learn from edits, and tighten autonomy thresholds over time. That's an agent loop, not a sequence.

## Stack pattern that works

Many of the buyers we talk to run Smartlead for warm-up and inbox-placement infrastructure, and the Sales Agent boilerplate for the reasoning + HITL layer. The agent drafts, a human approves, the draft gets handed to Smartlead for delivery. Both tools, each in their lane.
