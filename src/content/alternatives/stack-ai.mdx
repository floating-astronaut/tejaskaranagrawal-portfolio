## Different audiences entirely

Stack AI sells into enterprise IT — formal procurement, compliance attestations, central platform admin. Glitch Grow sells to agencies and indie devs — fast deploys, source ownership, per-client white-label.

If you're evaluating both, the question to ask is which audience your operating shape matches. There's no overlap large enough to make this a like-for-like comparison.
