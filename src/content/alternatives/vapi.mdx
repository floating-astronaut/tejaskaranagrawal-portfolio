## The voice market split

Voice AI right now divides into two camps:

1. **Polished platforms** — Vapi, Bland, Retell. Easy to start, premium per-minute pricing, vendor lock-in.
2. **Open stacks** — LiveKit + STT + LLM + TTS, assembled. Lower cost, more setup, full control.

Glitch Grow's Voice AI Agent is the second camp, pre-assembled. You skip the integration work that pushes most teams toward camp #1 in the first place.

## When to stay on Vapi

If your volume is low (say under 500 minutes/month per client) and your audience is English-only, the price difference rarely justifies switching. If your client is an Indian D2C brand running thousands of COD-confirm calls in Hindi and Tamil, the gap is unmissable.
