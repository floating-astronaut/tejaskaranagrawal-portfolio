## Where Zapier still earns its place

Zapier is excellent at low-volume cross-app glue: form submission → CRM row → Slack ping. If that's your workload, keep using it. The arbitrage breaks down once you're billing a client a managed-service retainer for an outcome — at that point you're paying Zapier per task to deliver something Zapier itself doesn't deliver, namely an agent that reasons, queues for approval, and learns.

## Why agencies switch

Three patterns repeat in conversations with buyers:

1. **Per-client cost scaling.** Zapier seats and task counts grow with each client. Glitch Grow's $499 is fixed; the marginal cost is infrastructure ($40–$150/mo single-VM) and your time.
2. **HITL is missing.** Outbound, ads changes, and ORM responses need a human in the loop on day one and lighter approval guardrails over time. Zapier has no approval queue primitive — buyers end up wiring their own through Slack channels and Google Sheets.
3. **Resale isn't ergonomic.** Zapier's white-label story (Zapier Embed, paid add-on) is built for SaaS embedding, not for an agency rebranding a managed service to a client.

## What you trade

Glitch Grow is code, not a connector marketplace. You're responsible for deploys, secret management, and uptime — none of which Zapier asks of you. If you don't have someone comfortable with `docker compose up` and `.env` files, the AI Digital Marketing Stack is the wrong starting point. We'd rather you keep that $499.
