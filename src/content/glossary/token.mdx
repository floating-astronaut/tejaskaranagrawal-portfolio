## Why token counts matter

- **Pricing** — APIs charge per million input/output tokens at different rates
- **Rate limits** — usually expressed as tokens/minute and tokens/day
- **Context window** — total budget per call

## Estimating quickly

For English text: ~4 characters per token, ~0.75 words per token. Code and non-English text tokenize less efficiently. Use the [Prompt Cost Estimator](/tools/prompt-cost-estimator) for monthly projections.
